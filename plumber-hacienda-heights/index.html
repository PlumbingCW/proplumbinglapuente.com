Hills, pressure and what they do to pipes

Streets that climb the Puente Hills can bring higher pressure to lower homes and weaker pressure to higher ones. High pressure wears faucets, hoses and appliance valves and can cause a burst. Low pressure makes showers weak and can point to a partly closed valve or a failing regulator.
