Garbage Disposal Cost and Installation for La Puente, CA Homeowners

The price of a garbage disposal is two prices: the unit and the labor to put it in. This guide shows what moves each one so you can compare quotes with confidence.

Written by La Puente Pro Plumbing · Updated November 2026

If you are weighing the cost of a garbage disposal and its installation in La Puente, start by splitting the bill in two. One part is the unit itself, which ranges from a basic quarter horsepower model to a heavy duty one with sound insulation. The other part is labor and materials, which depend on what is under your sink right now.

A like-for-like swap of a disposal that is already wired and plumbed is a short job. A first-time installation, with a new outlet or switch, a dishwasher connection and drain changes, takes longer and costs more. Quotes that look far apart usually describe different jobs, not different prices for the same job.

The unit

What you pay for in the disposal itself

Horsepower is the headline spec. Lower horsepower units handle everyday scraps for a small household. Higher horsepower units grind more, run with less strain and tend to be quieter, especially when they have extra insulation. Feed type matters too. A continuous feed unit runs while you hold the switch, and a batch feed unit runs only when a stopper is in place.

Mounting style, grind components and warranty length also change the price. A longer warranty usually comes with a heavier build. Ask your plumber to explain why they recommend a given unit for your household, not just which one is in stock.

The labor

What drives the installation labor

Replacing an old unit with a new one that has a matching mount is the simplest case. The plumber removes the old unit, cleans the sink flange area, mounts the new one, reconnects the drain and tests for leaks. If the mount style differs, the sink flange or mounting assembly may need to be changed too.

A first-time installation adds steps. The plumber needs power at the sink, either through an outlet under the cabinet or a switched connection. If the dishwasher drains through the disposal, its hose connects to an inlet on the unit, and the knockout plug inside that inlet must be removed. Skipping that step makes the dishwasher fail to drain. If wiring is needed, ask who is doing it and whether it is permitted.

Drain work

Drain and trap changes that add to the quote

Disposals need a proper trap and a drain that slopes toward the wall. Older sink drains are sometimes the wrong height or made of corroded pipe, and the plumber may need to replace the trap arm or adjust the connection. If the kitchen drain is slow before installation, a disposal will not fix it. It will push more material into a line that is already struggling.

In that case, clearing the line first is the right move, and drain cleaning covers how it is done. A new disposal on a clogged line can fail within weeks.

Repair first

When repair beats replacement

A disposal that hums and will not turn is often jammed, not dead. Freeing the flywheel and pressing the reset button can bring it back. A leak at a connection can be fixed by tightening or resealing. A leak from the body of the unit cannot be repaired and means replacement.

A plumber can tell you in a short visit which case you have. Our page on garbage disposal repair walks through the common failures and the usual fixes.

Habits

Use habits that protect the new unit

Run cold water while the unit is on and for a few seconds after. Feed scraps in small amounts instead of one large load. Keep grease, fibrous stalks, large bones and coffee grounds in bulk out of the unit, because they cause the clogs that bring plumbers to kitchens. A little citrus peel or ice helps with odors but does not clean the drain line.

What to know in La Puente before you install

The City of La Puente Building and Safety Division handles building, electrical, mechanical and plumbing permits. If your installation adds a new outlet or changes the wiring, ask that office whether a permit applies. Rules vary by scope, so check before the work starts and keep the paperwork if one is issued.

Hard water is another local factor. The La Puente Valley County Water District reported an average of 224 mg/L hardness in its 2023 report, and scale from hard water builds on dishwasher connections and flanges. Check your bill to see who supplies your address, then wipe the splash guard and check the dishwasher drain for buildup from time to time.

Disposal drains follow the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the power connection follows the California Electrical Code, based on the National Electrical Code (NEC).
