Cost of a New Toilet Installed in La Puente, CA

A new toilet is two purchases: the fixture and the installation. The flange and the floor under it often decide how the second part goes.

Written by La Puente Pro Plumbing · Updated November 2026

Replacing a toilet looks simple, and often it is. But the price depends on the toilet you choose, the condition of the flange and floor under it and whether the old unit comes out cleanly. Knowing these pieces helps you compare quotes.

This guide covers the fixture, the parts that come with installation, the problems that add work and what a good quote includes. It does not quote a dollar figure, because the right number depends on your bathroom.

The fixture

Choosing the toilet itself

Toilets differ in bowl shape, height, flush system and water use. Comfort height models sit taller. Elongated bowls are larger than round ones. Two-piece toilets have a separate tank and bowl, and one-piece toilets are sleeker and easier to clean but heavier.

Check the rough-in, the distance from the wall to the center of the drain. Most are twelve inches, but older bathrooms can differ. A toilet that does not match the rough-in will not fit, so measure before you buy.

Parts

What comes with installation

A new wax ring or seal, closet bolts, a new supply line and often a new shutoff valve are standard. Reusing an old supply line or a worn valve is a false saving. The plumber also sets the toilet level and caulks the base if appropriate.

Ask whether these parts are included in the quote or priced separately.

Surprises

What can add to the job

A cracked or corroded flange, a flange that sits below the finished floor and a rotted subfloor from a long leak all add work. Seized bolts and stuck old toilets can too. A plumber can usually tell you which case you have once the old toilet is lifted.

If the flange needs repair, a repair ring or a replacement flange restores a proper seat. Toilet repair covers flange issues in more detail.

Labor

How the labor is priced

Labor depends on access, the condition of the old toilet and any extra work. Removing and hauling away the old fixture, installing the new one and testing for leaks is a standard scope. Ask whether disposal is included.

If you are replacing several fixtures at once, say so. Multiple fixtures in a single visit often save trip time. Plumbing installation explains how fixture projects are scoped.

Quote

What a good quote lists

It names the toilet or lets you supply your own, lists the seal, bolts, supply line and valve, says whether removal and disposal are included and explains what happens if the flange or floor needs work. A written scope beats a number over the phone.

Preparation

How to get the bathroom ready for the plumber

Clear the space around the toilet so the plumber can work without moving boxes and cabinets. If you bought the new toilet, open the box and check for cracks, chips and missing parts before the visit. Keep the manual and the mounting hardware together.

Know where the shutoff valve is and check that it turns. If it does not, tell the plumber when you book so the visit allows time to replace it. Plan to have the bathroom out of use for a few hours, and ask the plumber how long the seal and any caulk need before you put full use back on the toilet.

After installation, flush several times and watch the base and the supply connection for weeping. Check again the next day with a dry paper towel pressed around the base. A slow leak shows up as a damp spot long before it shows up as a stain, and catching it early is far cheaper than replacing a swollen subfloor.

Local notes for La Puente toilet projects

The La Puente Valley County Water District reported a pH of 7.9 and average hardness of 224 mg/L in its 2023 report. Scale from hard water collects on fill valves and flush parts, so choose a toilet with parts that are easy to service. Check your bill to see who supplies your address.

For permits on larger bathroom projects, ask the City of La Puente Building and Safety Division, which handles building, electrical, mechanical and plumbing permits. A simple replacement may differ from a remodel.
