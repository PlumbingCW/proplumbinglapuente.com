Emergency plumbing

What counts as a plumbing emergency in La Puente

Call the emergency plumber right away for sewage backing up into a tub or floor drain, water pouring from a pipe or ceiling, a leaking water heater, a toilet that will not stop overflowing or water near electrical outlets. These problems get worse by the minute, and the 24/7 line exists for them.

A gas smell is different. Leave the building and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 from outside before you call a plumber. Never look for a gas leak with a flame.

Plumbing in California is built to the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and local building departments decide which permits a job needs. We point you to the right office on each page and do not guess at rules we could not confirm.

For water problems, the first move is the main shutoff valve, usually near the street side of the house or in the garage. Turn it clockwise until it stops, then call. Our pages on burst pipe repair and drain cleaning list what else to do while you wait.

Local homes

Why older La Puente plumbing fails the way it does

La Puente incorporated in 1956, and the former Skyranch airfield closed in 1951 to make way for homes. Much of the city's housing comes from the postwar decades, and the pipes in those houses are now old. Galvanized steel corrodes from the inside, early copper pits and clay sewer laterals let roots in.

That is why leaks, repeat clogs and low pressure are common calls here. If one failure is followed by another, ask the plumber to look at the bigger picture. Leak detection, sewer camera inspection and repiping each answer a different part of it.

Hard water

Hard water, scale and what it does to your plumbing

The La Puente Valley County Water District reported average treated-water hardness of 224 mg/L as calcium carbonate in its 2023 water quality report, with a range from 169 to 344 mg/L. That is hard water. Scale builds on heating elements, faucet aerators, fill valves and inside water heaters.

Not every address in the city is served by that district, so check your water bill for your provider. If scale is a regular problem, water softener and filtration explains the options, and water heater replacement covers how scale shortens tank life.
