Privacy Policy

This site has no forms, no accounts and no sign-up. Here is what that means for your information.

Talk to a plumber

This policy covers the website proplumbinglapuente.com and the phone line listed on it. It was last updated on November 13, 2026.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can arrange service. When you email, we receive the address you send from and whatever you include. We use that information to respond and to schedule and carry out the work.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to arrange the service you ask for, such as with the plumber who attends.

The website

What the site itself does

The website has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts, and the maps on the site are Google Maps embeds, which may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

Your choices

Questions and requests

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, email contact@proplumbinglapuente.com. We respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Our service area

We dispatch from La Puente and cover West Covina, Baldwin Park, Hacienda Heights, Rowland Heights and Valinda. Call to confirm your street.
