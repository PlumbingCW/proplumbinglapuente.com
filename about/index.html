La Puente Pro Plumbing is based at 14316 Amar Rd in La Puente, California. We serve La Puente and nearby communities including West Covina, Baldwin Park, Hacienda Heights, Rowland Heights and Valinda.

The business is built around one idea: when a pipe fails, you should reach a person, get a straight answer about what to do next and hear the price before anything starts.

How it works

What happens when you call

The dispatcher asks what you see, tells you what to do while you wait, such as closing the main water valve, and arranges a licensed plumber. You are given a realistic arrival window and not a fixed promise, because traffic and other calls change the schedule.

On site, the plumber finds the cause, explains the options in plain words and gives the price. You decide whether to go ahead. Our service pages describe each type of job in more detail.

What we do not claim

What you will not find on this site

You will not find invented reviews, star ratings, license numbers or named staff on this site. We list only what we can stand behind: the address, the phone number, the services and the areas we cover. If you want to verify a plumber's license, ask for the license number at the visit and check it with the California Contractors State License Board.

We do not take forms or messages through the site. The phone is the fastest route in an emergency, and it is the route we answer.

Where we work

Our service area

We dispatch from La Puente and cover the surrounding towns. See service areas for the list, or call and give the dispatcher your address.

What we handle

The work we take on

Emergency calls make up the heart of the business: drain cleaning, burst pipe repair, water heater repair, leak detection and sewer line repair. Plumbing work is done to the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Alongside the emergency work sit planned jobs such as repiping, plumbing installation and commercial plumbing.

Each service page says what the problem looks like, what the options are and what drives the price. We write them that way because a customer who understands the choices makes a better decision than one who is rushed.

Local facts

How we handle local information

Plumbing rules and water quality are local. When this site quotes a number, such as water hardness reported by the La Puente Valley County Water District for 2023, it names the source. When we could not confirm a rule, such as a permit requirement, we say to ask the City of La Puente Building and Safety Division or your water utility.

That habit costs us a little polish and gives you something more useful, which is a pointer to the office that decides.

Our promises

What we do promise

A person answers the phone at any hour. You hear the price before work starts. The plumber explains what failed and why. Arrival windows are honest estimates and not fixed promises, because traffic and other calls change the schedule. If a repair is not the best answer, the plumber says so and shows you the alternative.

Hiring any plumber

Questions worth asking before you hire a plumber

Whoever you call, ask for the contractor license number and check it with the California Contractors State License Board before work begins. Ask for the price in writing or by text, and ask what could change it. Ask who will attend and how the plumber is insured. Ask what the work is warrantied for, and get the answer in writing.

These questions apply to us as much as anyone else. A plumber who welcomes them is usually the one worth hiring, and a plumber who hurries you past them is telling you something too.
