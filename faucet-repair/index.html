Faucet repair in La Puente covers the common failures: a steady drip from the spout, water at the base of the handle, low flow from a clogged aerator and a faucet that is loose on the sink. Most are fixed in one visit.

The plumber also decides with you whether repair or replacement is smarter. A worn cartridge in a good faucet is a cheap repair. A pitted, corroded faucet with parts that are no longer made is better replaced.

Before the plumber arrives

What to do before the plumber arrives

A few minutes of preparation saves time on site and limits damage if something goes wrong while the old part comes out.

  • Find and test the shutoff valves under the sink or behind the fixture, and tell us if any will not turn.
  • Clear the cabinet or the space around the fixture so the plumber can reach it.
  • Have the brand and model number, or a photo of the fixture, ready.
  • Know where the main water valve is in case a valve fails when it is disturbed.
  • Put towels and a bucket nearby, and keep children and pets out of the work area.
Drips

Why faucets drip

A drip from the spout usually means a worn seat, washer, O-ring or cartridge. Hard water speeds up that wear by leaving scale on the moving parts. Replacing the worn part stops the drip. A faucet that drips from the spout and handle together may need a full cartridge.

Different faucet types fail differently. Compression faucets wear washers, cartridge and ball faucets wear seals and cartridges, and ceramic disc faucets wear discs. Identifying the type is the first step in the repair.

Leaks

Leaks at the handle, base or under the sink

Water at the handle points to worn O-rings or a cartridge. Water at the base can come from a loose mount or a failed seal. Water under the sink may come from supply lines, shutoff valves or the drain trap rather than the faucet itself.

Dry the area, run the water and watch where the drip starts. If a supply line or valve is leaking, the plumber replaces it. For bigger bursts, see burst pipe repair.

Flow

Low flow and noisy faucets

Low flow often means a clogged aerator or debris in the cartridge. Unscrew the aerator and rinse it. If flow is still weak, the supply valves, lines or cartridge may be restricted. Noise or vibration can come from loose parts or high pressure.

If several fixtures show low flow, the cause may be the main supply. That is a different problem, covered under water line repair.

Replace

When to replace the faucet

Replace a faucet when it is corroded, when parts are no longer available or when the cost of repair approaches the cost of a new unit. A new faucet also gives you a chance to choose a style, spray or touchless feature.

Installation is covered in plumbing installation. The plumber checks the shutoff valves at the same time, since old valves often fail when disturbed.

Faucets in La Puente homes

The La Puente Valley County Water District reported average hardness of 224 mg/L in 2023, which is hard water. Scale builds up on aerators, cartridges and seats, so drips and weak flow are common in the area.

Cleaning aerators every few months helps. If you are tired of scale, see water softener and filtration for options.

Faucets and supply stops follow the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Replacing a faucet is a like-for-like job. If a repair changes the pipe layout, ask the City of La Puente Building and Safety Division about permits.
