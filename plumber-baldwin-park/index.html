Common plumbing calls in Baldwin Park

Multi-unit and shops

Housing age

Postwar homes and the pipes inside them

Baldwin Park's housing stock leans toward homes built in the postwar boom. Those houses were plumbed with galvanized steel or early copper, and sewer laterals of clay or cast iron. After decades in the ground, steel corrodes from the inside, copper pits and clay joints open to roots.

A pattern of small leaks or discolored water is a sign the supply pipes are tiring. Repiping explains when replacement beats patching. A string of clogs points to the lateral, covered under sewer line repair.
