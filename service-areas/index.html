How we decide where we can go

We dispatch from 14316 Amar Rd in La Puente. The five towns on this page are the closest communities with a short drive, and each has local plumbing patterns of its own. Valinda adjoins La Puente to the north. Baldwin Park lies to the northwest, West Covina to the northeast, Hacienda Heights to the south and Rowland Heights to the southeast.

Plumbing in every one of these towns follows the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and local building offices decide the permits. Local differences

Why each town gets its own page

Plumbing problems follow housing and terrain. Hillside streets in Hacienda Heights bring pressure questions. Busy commercial plazas in Rowland Heights bring grease and drain calls. Older postwar streets in Baldwin Park and West Covina bring aging laterals and supply lines, and compact lots in Valinda make cleanouts easy to find.

Water providers also differ by address. Check your bill for your provider, and ask that provider for its latest water quality report. We name the sources we used on each town page and do not guess at utility details we could not confirm.

When you call

What to tell the dispatcher from any of these towns

Give your street address and the nearest cross street, say what you see happening and tell the dispatcher whether you have closed the main water valve. Mention if the property is a house, an apartment or a business, because the equipment sent can differ. If you rent, say so, since the landlord may need to be told in writing.

If you are not sure which provider supplies your water, check your bill. The provider decides where its pipes end and yours begin, which matters for a leak near the meter. The dispatcher can talk you through the first steps while the plumber is on the way.
